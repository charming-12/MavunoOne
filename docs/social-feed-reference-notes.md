## MavunoOne catalog decision

- `Pumba` / `Maize Bran` gets its own product-specific image.
- `Chokaa` remains its own mineral/feed-ingredient product with its own image.
- `Soya`, `Uduv`, `Mashudu` and poultry/dairy feeds should each use distinct images when the product record provides them.
- The social posts are references only; do not copy their logos, phone numbers, prices, or branded packaging into MavunoOne.
